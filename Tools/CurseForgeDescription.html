💥 Forever Enhanced Cooldown Pulse

💬 Support, bugs & ideas: join our Discord to get help, report issues and suggest features.

A big icon in the middle of your screen the moment a cooldown is ready, on World of Warcraft: Forever. Tick the spells, trinkets and potions you care about, and each one pops up as it comes back, in a fight too.

✦ Highlights

  • 💥 The pulse: a big icon pops up in the middle of your screen as a cooldown you ticked comes back, then fades away. It never takes the mouse and works in a fight
  • 📜 Your cooldowns, listed for you: every spell you know with a cooldown of its own, at your highest rank. New ones show up as you learn them, ready to tick
  • 🧪 Trinkets and potions: trinkets with a use, healthstones and potions (the best one you carry), and other items with a use in your bags
  • ⚡ Quick and Long: pick a style for each cooldown. Each style has its own size, time and sound
  • 🔔 15 sounds from the game, played with your Sound Effects or at your Master volume
  • 🎨 Your look: how see-through it is, how big it grows, a border and a soft shadow
  • 📍 Move it anywhere, or Reset it to the middle, with a live preview and a Preview button
  • 🤝 Works with Forever Enhanced Cooldown Manager: only one pulse runs at a time, and this one takes over as soon as that one is turned off, no reload needed
  • 👥 Profiles: each character keeps its own picks, or share one across characters
  • 🎓 A quick tour the first time you log in, and a ? on every page that walks you through it
  • 🗺️ Minimap button: click it for the settings, drag it round the minimap, or hide it

✦ Getting started

  1. Log in: the settings open by themselves the first time, with a quick tour you can skip
  2. Tick the cooldowns you want to pulse, and pick Quick or Long for each
  3. After that, click the minimap button or type /fecp (also under Options > AddOns)
