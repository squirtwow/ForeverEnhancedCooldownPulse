# Forever Enhanced Cooldown Pulse

A big icon in the middle of your screen the moment a cooldown is ready, on
**World of Warcraft: Forever**. Tick the spells, trinkets and potions you care
about, and each one pops up as it comes back, in a fight too. The same pulse as
the Cooldown pulse page in Forever Enhanced Cooldown Manager, as an addon of its
own.

## The pulse
- On from the start: tick your cooldowns on the Cooldown pulse page and they
  pulse as they come back
- Every spell you know with a cooldown of its own is listed, at your highest
  rank, and new ones show as you learn them
- Trinkets with a use, healthstones and potions (the best one you carry), and
  other items with a use in your bags
- Several at once take turns, so it never floods your screen
- It never takes the mouse, and works in a fight

## Quick and Long
- Each cooldown pulses Quick or Long, picked on its row
- Each style has its own size, time and sound
- 15 sounds from the game, played with Sound Effects or at your Master volume
- See-through, how big it grows, a border and a soft shadow for both
- A live preview on the page, and Preview to see one on your screen
- Move it anywhere, or Reset it to the middle

## With Forever Enhanced Cooldown Manager
- Only one pulse runs at a time. With both on, Forever Enhanced Cooldown
  Manager's runs, and this one's tick is greyed out, saying where the pulse
  runs and how to swap
- This one takes over as soon as that one is turned off. No reload needed

## Profiles and settings
- What's ticked, and which are Long, live in profiles. Each character gets its
  own, and characters can share one. Use on all characters puts one profile on
  every character
- An accent colour for the window, and What's new after each update
- Hover anything in /fecp and the footer tells you what it does, or click ? for
  a walkthrough of the page

## Getting around
- The first time you log in, the settings open with a quick tour of the basics
- A minimap button: click it for the settings, right-click for What's new, drag
  it round the minimap, or turn it off on the General page
- `/fecp` opens the settings; they're also under Options > AddOns
- `/fecp tour` takes the tour again, and `/fecp new` shows What's new again
- Found a bug or have an idea? The Discord button on the General page gives you
  the invite, or type `/fecp discord`
